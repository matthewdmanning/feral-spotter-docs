# Product

## Vision

Help someone who sees a feral cat record a usable Sighting while the encounter is still fresh, then send its photo, location, and cat details to the research database. This is the Phase 1 job statement, not a claim that every Sighting prompts rescue action.

## MVP Definition

Working audit boundary: capture photos, identify the cat in them, record what the observer knows, and submit one Sighting with one location. The dataset's required versus optional cat attributes still need a product decision (U22). Retention work is deferred.
